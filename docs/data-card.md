# RaceVault data card

## Datasets

`evaluation/queries.json` is the `racevault-corpus-v2` set over the private
64-document corpus: 40 queries, 31 answerable and 9 out-of-corpus, spanning nine
document families that are split development/test without crossing the boundary.
It is generated by `evaluation/build_dataset.py`, which upgrades the legacy set
in `queries-legacy-v1.json` and verifies every label against the indexed corpus.
Rebuild it whenever the corpus changes; a label that no longer matches a chunk
fails the build rather than silently scoring zero.

Labels are author-verified against source passages, not independently annotated.
Inter-annotator agreement is unmeasured and graded relevance is coarse, so these
numbers remain a regression baseline rather than a general retrieval claim.

`evaluation/queries-legacy-v1.json` preserves the original 16-query set: 15
answerable queries and one wrong-revision negative. It drew 7 of its 17 labels
from the 66-chunk tyre-data class and left component manuals, part catalogues,
and the engineering reference unrepresented.

`evaluation/public/queries-v2.example.json` demonstrates the v2 schema using
eight queries and four deterministic CC0 documents. The PDFs are generated from
`fixture_sources.json` and deliberately include revision conflicts, exact
identifiers, numeric units, a procedure, a table-like passage, an unanswerable
scope, and a document prompt-injection string.

The target `racevault-engineering-v2` study is 200 human-written queries: 140
development, 60 held-out test, at least 40 unanswerable, and 40 independently
double-labelled. `annotation_progress.json` is the source of truth for completion;
targets are not results.

## Label contract

Evidence is graded 1–3 and may have multiple acceptable passages. Each query
records its split, slices, document family, required source coverage, optional
atomic gold claims, expected abstention, and expected conflict. Document families
cannot cross development and test splits.

Question authors should work from realistic engineering tasks, not generate a
question by paraphrasing a selected target chunk. Annotators may search the
document only after recording the intended information need. Disagreements are
adjudicated and quadratic weighted kappa is reported.

## Governance

The existing PDF corpus is excluded from distribution unless its licence permits
redistribution. `corpus/public_sources.json` requires an official URL, content
hash, licence, redistribution decision, and review date before a realistic public
source is admitted. Unknown rights default to non-distribution.

Potential biases include a single engineering domain, English-language material,
document-family imbalance, and author familiarity with the retrieval system.
